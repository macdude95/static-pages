Cypher Melee Sets

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Cypher Melee Sets is a private, personal automation operated by Michael Cantrell. It is not offered to the public and does not provide accounts or services to other users.

Information accessed

With the owner’s authorization, the automation uses the YouTube Data API to read public video and channel information and to manage one playlist owned by the owner. It may access the playlist’s video IDs and add matching public videos.

How information is used

Information is used only to identify matching Melee videos and maintain the owner’s “Melee Sets” playlist. The automation does not sell, rent, or share YouTube data with third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner’s computer with restricted file permissions. No YouTube credentials or private account data are stored on this website.

Data retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the automation’s YouTube access at any time through Google Account security settings and can delete the local credentials. Public YouTube data remains subject to YouTube’s own policies.
